About BehaviourLog

BehaviourLog is available to any Behaviour Support Practitioner (BSP), Senior Practitioner, occupational therapist, or support worker in Australia who works within the NDIS framework. It is a general public professional app — not restricted to a single organisation. Any practitioner can register and begin using it independently.

Getting Started

Who is BehaviourLog for?

BehaviourLog is designed for: Behaviour Support Practitioners (BSPs), Senior Practitioners, Occupational Therapists working in assistive technology (AT) assessments, and support workers who implement Behaviour Support Plans (BSPs) in the field. Any qualified professional working in the NDIS behaviour support space can use it.

How do I access BehaviourLog?

Download from the App Store and tap "Continue with Demo Account" to explore all features without signing up. To save real client data, register with your professional email on the login screen.

Do I need to be registered with the NDIS Commission?

BehaviourLog is designed to assist practitioners who work under NDIS registration, but registration is not required to use the app. The app is a professional productivity tool — compliance with NDIS registration requirements is the responsibility of the practitioner.

Key Features

BSP Builder

Create structured Behaviour Support Plans with antecedent analysis, function-based strategies, and restrictive practice documentation. BSPs follow the PBS (Positive Behaviour Support) framework required by the NDIS Commission.

ABC Data Collection

Record Antecedent-Behaviour-Consequence (ABC) data with a single tap at the moment of an incident — not at the end of a shift. Fast data entry is critical for accurate behaviour tracking.

Incident Reporting

Generate NDIS Commission-compliant reportable incident forms. Incidents are documented with required fields aligned to NDIS reporting standards, ready to lodge or provide to your senior practitioner.

Analytics Dashboard

View behaviour frequency charts and trend analysis over time. Track whether strategies are reducing target behaviours. Export charts and reports for progress reporting.

Restrictive Practices Tracking

Document, review, and report on regulated and prohibited restrictive practices in compliance with NDIS Quality and Safeguarding Framework requirements.

OT Module — AT Assessments

Generate NDIS assistive technology (AT) assessment reports. Includes sensory profile assessments and an AT recommendation engine that produces "reasonable and necessary" justifications for AT funding requests.

Privacy and Data Security

How is client data protected?

All client data is stored locally on your device and in your private iCloud account (if iCloud is enabled). BehaviourLog does not transmit data to any external server. See our full Privacy Policy.
