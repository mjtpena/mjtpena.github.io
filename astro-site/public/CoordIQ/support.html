About CoordIQ

CoordIQ is designed for NDIS Support Coordinators and Specialist Support Coordinators in Australia. It is a professional tool available to any qualified support coordination practitioner — not limited to any single organisation. Subscriptions are managed externally at andriel.net.

Getting Started

How do I access CoordIQ?

Sign up for a subscription at andriel.net, then log in with your registered email and password. A demo account is also available in the app — tap "Continue with Demo Account" on the login screen to explore features without signing up.

What subscription plans are available?

Solo — $149/month: Up to 40 active participants, all core features.
Practice — $349/month: Unlimited participants, up to 3 coordinators, team collaboration.
Enterprise: Agency-wide licensing — contact us for pricing.

Where do I purchase a subscription?

Subscriptions are purchased directly at andriel.net via the web — not through the App Store. This is standard practice for professional B2B SaaS tools used in enterprise and healthcare settings.

Can I use CoordIQ as an individual coordinator?

Yes. The Solo plan is designed for individual support coordinators running their own practice. Enterprise plans are sold to agencies, but individual practitioners can subscribe independently.

Key Features

AI Note Writer

Dictate or type bullet points about a support session and CoordIQ generates a complete, NDIS-compliant progress note. Notes can be exported as PDF or copied for your NDIS portal.

Caseload Dashboard

See all your participants' plan status, active goals, budget utilisation, and next required actions in a single view. Filter by urgency or plan review date.

Task Management

Create follow-up tasks linked to specific participants and goals. Tasks include due dates and can be exported to your calendar.

Budget Tracking

Track funding utilisation across Core Supports, Capacity Building, and Capital Supports for each participant. Receive alerts when a participant approaches budget limits.

Provider Directory

Search the NDIS registered provider directory by support category, location, and registration group. Contact providers directly from within the app.

NDIS Compliance

Is CoordIQ aligned with NDIS Practice Standards?

Yes. CoordIQ is built to align with the NDIS Practice Standards for Support Coordination. Progress notes follow NDIS audit trail requirements and support category tracking uses current NDIS support categories.

Is participant data stored securely?

Data is stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your private iCloud account. CoordIQ does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Troubleshooting

I forgot my password

Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login screen. A reset link will be sent to your registered email.

My subscription isn't activating

After purchasing at andriel.net, log out and log back in to refresh your subscription status. If the issue persists, contact us at mj@andriel.net with your order confirmation.

Data isn't syncing between devices

Ensure both devices are signed into the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive enabled. Go to Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → iCloud Drive to verify.
