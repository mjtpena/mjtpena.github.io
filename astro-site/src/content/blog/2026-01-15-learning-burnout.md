I wrote "The Myth of Continuous Learning" to share practical, production-minded guidance on this topic.

## The Pressure

New framework every month. New AI model every week. "10x developers" learning constantly.

The message: If you're not learning something new constantly, you're falling behind.

## The Reality

Learning fatigue is real. You can't absorb new information endlessly without burnout.

## What Actually Works

**Deep, not broad.** Master what you use. Don't dabble in everything.

**Gaps, not continuous.** Sprint, then rest. Learn, then consolidate.

**Relevant, not trendy.** Learn what helps your work, not what's hyped.

## Permission to Stop

You don't need to learn the new JavaScript framework. Or the latest AI model. Or that new database.

Focus on depth in your stack. Ignore the rest.
