## What 2024 Taught Us

### Lesson 1: AI is Real, Not Hype

```
The shift from "AI is coming" to "AI is here":

2023: "Should we use AI?"
2024: "How do we use AI effectively?"
2025: "How do we do anything without AI?"

Key realization: AI is now a required skill, not a nice-to-have.
```
